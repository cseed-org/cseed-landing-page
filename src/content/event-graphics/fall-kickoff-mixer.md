The first gathering of the year and the easiest one to walk into cold. We keep the
programming light: a short welcome around 6pm, then open time to talk to people
running each working group.

## What to expect

- **Doors 5:30pm.** Come and go as you like; nothing is sequential.
- **Introductions at 6:00pm.** Ten minutes, then back to open floor.
- **Working group tables** for each of our active projects.

Dinner is provided. If you have dietary restrictions, note them in the RSVP form and
we will make sure there is something for you.
