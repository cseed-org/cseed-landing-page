Dinner first, conversation second. We seat people in groups of six with a trained
facilitator at each table, and everyone gets the questions in advance so nobody is
put on the spot.

## Format

1. **6:00pm — Dinner.** Catered, vegetarian and halal options available.
2. **6:45pm — Table conversation.** Roughly an hour, facilitated.
3. **7:45pm — Closing.** Brief share-back, then out by 8:00pm.

Because seating is capped, please cancel your RSVP if your plans change so someone on
the waitlist can take the spot.
